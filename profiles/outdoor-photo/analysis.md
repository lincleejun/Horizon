# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much an item is worth the time of an enthusiast who shoots wildlife and landscape photography, follows camera gear closely, and hikes and backpacks.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Must read.** Major camera or lens launches, significant firmware or autofocus changes, landmark wildlife or expedition stories, or an exceptional field guide from a recognized practitioner.
- **7-8: High value.** Thorough hands-on reviews, well-sourced gear rumors with specifications, practical technique articles with concrete settings or fieldcraft, major competition results, and notable trail, route, or access news.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental gear updates, good photo essays, useful but familiar tips, and routine trip reports.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Thin rumors, minor accessories, listicles, and restated press releases.
- **0-2: Noise.** Deals, affiliate roundups, sponsored posts, giveaways, or off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider practical usefulness in the field, originality, the author's first-hand experience, and concrete detail such as specifications, settings, locations, or conditions. Score rumors by the track record of the source and the specificity of the claim, not by how exciting it sounds. Penalize content whose main purpose is to drive purchases.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
