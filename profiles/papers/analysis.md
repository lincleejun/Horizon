# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a research paper is worth the attention of a practitioner who builds with AI systems and follows the research frontier, judging from the abstract and supplied metadata.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** A new capability, architecture, or result likely to change practice or redirect research, from credible authors with strong evidence.
- **7-8: High value.** A clear, well-evidenced advance, a practically useful method with released code or models, or a rigorous evaluation that changes how results should be read.
- **5-6: Solid.** A sound incremental improvement or a useful benchmark or survey with a narrow audience.
- **3-4: Minor.** Small gains on a familiar setup, limited evaluation, or unclear novelty.
- **0-2: Noise.** Unsupported claims, off-topic work, or content too thin to assess.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider novelty, strength of evidence, practical applicability, and whether code, models, or data are released. Treat community upvotes and repository stars as a signal of interest, not of correctness. Do not reward a bold abstract claim that the described experiments cannot support.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
